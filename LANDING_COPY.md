## FAQ

**Q: Is this really free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial period, no "surprise billing in 7 days." We built this because most startups don't realize they're overpaying for AI tools. If your audit shows significant savings, we'll optionally connect you with Credex for discounted AI credits — but that's entirely your choice.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: Every price in our audit engine is pulled directly from official vendor pricing pages and verified as of May 2026. We cite our sources. The savings numbers are real, defensible, and based on plan-to-plan comparisons. We don't invent discounts or exaggerate.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Your audit results are saved so you can access the shareable link later. If you choose to provide your email for the full report, we store it securely. We never share individual audit data with third parties. Your company's spending is your business.

**Q: What's the catch? Why did Credex build this?**
A: No catch. Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. We built this tool because we noticed most companies don't know they're overspending — they just pay their bills and move on. If your audit shows real savings, and you want to capture them through discounted credits, we'd love to help. If your audit shows you're already optimized, we'll tell you that honestly and notify you if new optimizations become available. Either way, you win.

**Q: What tools do you support?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Windsurf, Anthropic API, and OpenAI API. We update pricing weekly. If your tool isn't listed, it's probably on our roadmap — or you can suggest it.

**Q: Can I run this for my whole company?**
A: Absolutely. The audit works for teams of 1 to 500+. If you're auditing a larger organization, we recommend running the audit once for your typical team configuration and extrapolating — or contact us for a custom consult.

**Q: What if I only use free tools?**
A: Then you're already winning, and we'll tell you that. The tool won't manufacture savings where there aren't any. But if you ever add paid tools or upgrade plans, come back and re-run it — vendors change pricing constantly.
